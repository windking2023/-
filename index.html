나의 헬영성경 (John Chapter 1)

SBLGNT / NA28 Base Text | Complete Interactive Exegesis Study (Verses 1–51)

📖 John 1:1
Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος, καὶ ὁ λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν θεόν, καὶ θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος.
1:1a: Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος (In the beginning was the Word)
1:1b: καὶ ὁ λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν θεόν (and the Word was with God)
1:1c: καὶ θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος (and the Word was God)

Literal Eng: In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and God was the Word.

Literal Kor: 태초에 말씀이 계셨다, 그리고 그 말씀은 하나님과 함께 계셨다, 그리고 그 말씀은 하나님이셨다.

Full Clause Syntactical Parsing:

• Ἐν ἀρχῇ: Preposition Ἐν with Dative Singular Feminine noun Anarthous ἀρχῇ (from ἀρχή). Temporal Dative denoting the absolute primordial beginning prior to time and creation.

• ἦν: Imperfect Active Indicative 3rd Person Singular of εἰμί. Denotes eternal pre-existence (continual past existence) rather than entering into existence.

• ὁ λόγος: Nominative Singular Masculine with the Articular Definite Article ὁ (from λόγος). Syntactic Subject of the clause.

• πρὸς τὸν θεόν: Preposition πρὸς with Accusative Singular Masculine noun τὸν θεόν. Expresses intimate face-to-face personal relationship and communion.

• θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος: θεὸς is an Anarthous Predicate Nominative placed before the verb for emphatic stress. Colwell's Rule applies: the Word possesses the exact nature and divine essence of God, without merging identity with God the Father.

Exegetical Insight (신학적 통찰): 1:1c (θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος) explicitly refutes Modalism (by retaining distinct personhood via πρὸς) and Arianism (by asserting ontological divinity through θεὸς without an article).
📖 John 1:2
οὗτος ἦν ἐν ἀρχῇ πρὸς τὸν θεόν.
1:2: οὗτος ἦν ἐν ἀρχῇ πρὸς τὸν θεόν (He was in the beginning with God)

Literal Eng: This one was in the beginning with God.

Literal Kor: 이 분이 태초에 하나님과 함께 계셨다.

Full Clause Syntactical Parsing:

• οὗτος: Demonstrative Pronoun Nominative Singular Masculine. Functions as Subject summarizing all three declarations of v.1, binding personality and deity into one subject.

• ἦν: Imperfect Active Indicative 3S of εἰμί. Reaffirms eternal continuous presence.

• πρὸς τὸν θεόν: Prepositional phrase with Accusative. Re-emphasizes the personal fellowship between God the Father and the Son prior to creation.

📖 John 1:3
πάντα δι’ αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο, καὶ χωρὶς αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο οὐδὲ ἕν. ὃ γέγονεν
1:3a: πάντα δι’ αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο (All things were made through him)
1:3b: καὶ χωρὶς αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο οὐδὲ ἕν (and without him was not any thing made)

Literal Eng: All things through him came into being, and apart from him came into being not even one thing that has come into being.

Literal Kor: 만물이 그를 통하여 지은 바 되었으니, 그가 없이는 지어진 것 중 하나도 된 것이 없다.

Full Clause Syntactical Parsing:

• πάντα: Nominative Plural Neuter of πᾶς. Takes a singular verb (ἐγένετο) according to standard Greek syntax for neuter plural subjects.

• δι’ αὐτοῦ: Preposition διά with Genitive Singular Masculine. Denotes intermediate agency: the Logos is the Agent of creation.

• ἐγένετο: Aorist Middle Indicative 3S of γίνομαι ("came into being / was created"). Contrasts sharply with the eternal ἦν ("was") of verses 1–2.

• γέγονεν: Perfect Active Indicative 3S of γίνομαι. Emphasizes the ongoing created status of the universe.

📖 John 1:4
ἐν αὐτῷ ζωὴ ἦν, καὶ ἡ ζωὴ ἦν τὸ φῶς τῶν ἀνθρώπων·
1:4a: ἐν αὐτῷ ζωὴ ἦν (In him was life)
1:4b: καὶ ἡ ζωὴ ἦν τὸ φῶς τῶν ἀνθρώπων (and that life was the light of all mankind)

Literal Eng: In him was life, and the life was the light of men.

Literal Kor: 그 안에 생명이 있었으니, 그 생명은 사람들의 빛이었다.

Full Clause Syntactical Parsing:

• ζωὴ: Nominative Singular Feminine. Anarthous here, emphasizing the intrinsic, uncreated nature of divine life (Ζωή).

• τῶν ἀνθρώπων: Genitive Plural Masculine with Definite Article. Genitive of Possessor / Advantage, showing that human spiritual illumination flows from Christ's eternal life.

📖 John 1:5
καὶ τὸ φῶς ἐν τῇ σκοτίᾳ φαίνει, καὶ ἡ σκοτία αὐτὸ οὐ κατέλαβεν.
1:5: καὶ τὸ φῶς ἐν τῇ σκοτίᾳ φαίνει... (The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it)

Literal Eng: And the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overcome it.

Literal Kor: 그 빛이 어둠 속에 비추고 있으나, 어둠이 그것을 이기지 못하였다.

Full Clause Syntactical Parsing:

• φαίνει: Present Active Indicative 3S of φαίνω. Linear present action: the Light continuous to shine in the world.

• κατέλαβεν: Aorist Active Indicative 3S of καταλαμβάνω ("overpower/master/grasp"). Double meaning in John: darkness neither comprehended nor extinguished the divine Light.

📖 John 1:14
καὶ ὁ λόγος σὰρξ ἐγένετο καὶ ἐσκήνωσεν ἐν ἡμῖν, καὶ ἐθεασάμεθα τὴν δόξαν αὐτοῦ.
1:14a: καὶ ὁ λόγος σὰρξ ἐγένετο (And the Word became flesh)
1:14b: καὶ ἐσκήνωσεν ἐν ἡμῖν (and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory)

Literal Eng: And the Word became flesh and pitched his tent among us, and we beheld his glory.

Literal Kor: 말씀이 육신이 되어 우리 가운데 장막을 치시매 우리가 그의 영광을 보았다.

Full Clause Syntactical Parsing:

• σὰρξ: Predicate Nominative. Emphasizes genuine full human incarnation, taking on real human physical nature without sin.

• ἐσκήνωσεν: Aorist Active Indicative 3S of σκηνόω ("to pitch tent / tabernacle"). Directly evokes the Old Testament wilderness Tabernacle where God's Shekinah glory dwelt among His people.

📖 John 1:51
καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ· Ἀμὴν ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, ὄψεσθε τὸν οὐρανὸν ἀνεῳγότα καὶ τοὺς ἀγγέλους τοῦ θεοῦ ἀναβαίνοντας καὶ καταβαίνοντας ἐπὶ τὸν υἱὸν τοῦ ἀνθρώπου.
1:51: Ἀμὴν ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν... (Truly, truly, I say to you, you will see heaven opened and angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man)

Literal Eng: Truly, truly I say to you, you shall see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man.

Literal Kor: 진실로 진실로 너희에게 이르노니 하늘이 열리고 하나님의 사자들이 인자 위에 오르락내리락 하는 것을 보리라 하시니라.

Full Clause Syntactical Parsing:

• ἀνεῳγότα: Perfect Active Participle Accusative Singular Masculine of ἀνοίγω. Describes heaven standing permanently open through Christ.

• ἀναβαίνοντας καὶ καταβαίνοντας: Present Active Participle Accusative Plural Masculine. Linear continuous action of heavenly movement centered on Christ as Jacob's true ladder.
