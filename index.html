📌 [1:1a] Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος
Literal Eng: In the beginning was the Word
Literal Kor: 태초에 말씀이 계셨다
  • Ἐν (In /ɪn/): Preposition taking the Dative /ˈdeɪ.tɪv/ case.
  • ἀρχῇ (beginning /bɪˈɡɪn.ɪŋ/): Dative singular. Anarthous /ænˈɑːr.θrəs/ after a preposition.
  • ἦν (was /wɒz/): Imperfect /ɪmˈpɜː.fɪkt/ active indicative 3rd person singular of εἰμί.
  • ὁ λόγος (the Word /ðə wɜːd/): Nominative /ˈnɒm.ɪ.nə.tɪv/ singular masculine with article.
Exegetical Insight: The imperfect tense ἦν emphasizes that when "the beginning" occurred, the Word was already in existence.

Exegetical Insight: Reaffirms the eternal fellowship between the Son and the Father before creation.

📌 [1:3a] πάντα δι’ αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο
Literal Eng: All things came into being through Him
Literal Kor: 만물이 그를 통하여 지은 바 되었다
  • πάντα (all things): Nominative neuter plural of πᾶς. Neuter plural subject takes a singular verb.
  • ἐγένετο (came into being /keɪm ˈɪn.tuː ˈbiː.ɪŋ/): Aorist middle indicative 3rd person singular of γίνομαι.
  • γέγονεν (has come into being /hæz kʌm ˈɪn.tuː ˈbiː.ɪŋ/): Perfect active indicative 3rd person singular.
Exegetical Insight: Contrasts ἐγένετο (created origination) with ἦν (eternal uncreated existence) in verses 1-2.

Exegetical Insight: Divine life is inherent in the Word and functions as spiritual illumination for humanity.

Literal Eng: and the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overcome it
Literal Kor: 그리고 그 빛이 어둠 속에 비치고 있으며, 어둠이 그것을 이기지 못했다

Exegetical Insight: The light shines continuously (present tense), and darkness has never been able to overpower or extinguish it (aorist tense).
